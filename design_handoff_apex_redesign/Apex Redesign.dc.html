Apex · Redesign proposal · 29 Sept 2026

Review and new design direction

Apex already does the right things. The redesign reorders them: the morning question gets the top of the screen, planning lives in one place, and the chart shows fitness first. Same features, same data, fewer boxes.

01 · Review
Overview
Everything has the same weight
Today, stats, chart, week and insights are all equal cards. New Today screen: verdict and session first, then one Form row, then the week. Insights move to Season.
PMC chart
Five data layers on one axis
Default view: fitness line, forecast and race marker. Fatigue and form are toggles. TSS dots become thin bars in their own lane. The date inputs move into a "Custom" sheet.
Navigation
Three overlapping planning tabs
Calendar becomes the place to follow and move sessions. Plan is the library. Planner and Compare merge into Season. 6 tabs become 5.
Phone header
Wraps onto two lines
Logo, one sync status pill, avatar. Settings, Upload .FIT and Sign out live in the account sheet. Pull to refresh triggers a Strava sync.
Typography
Numbers read as code
One family (Geist) with tabular figures for every number. Mono is kept only for JSON, file names and the debug log. Dates are plain text.
Tone
"0 TSS" on Monday feels like failure
Show progress against "planned so far" (the rule calendar.js already uses: 25 / 25 on Monday evening), with remaining sessions as ghost segments. Amber only appears when you fall behind.
Surfaces
Card soup
Two surface levels, hairline dividers inside groups, a 4 px spacing scale. Borders only on inputs and planned-but-not-done items.
Icons and mobile tables
Emoji and sideways scrolling
Line icons only; Feel becomes a labelled 5-step scale. Log and Planner grid become lists on phones, tables from 900 px.

Rule of thumb: Calendar answers "what and when", Plan answers "how" (warm-up, sets, targets, .zwo / .fit), Season answers "where will I be on race day". Tapping a session anywhere opens the same session sheet. Settings, Upload .FIT, Disconnect Strava, debug log and Sign out move to the account sheet.
03 · Two directions
Recommendation: 1a as the default, with 1b shipped as the light theme that follows iOS appearance.
1a · Recommended
Graphite
Quiet, near-black instrument panel. The interface itself is monochrome, so the only colours on screen are data: fitness, fatigue, form, load and sport. Dense where it helps (tables, calendar), roomy on Today.
